# 캐릭터 그림 프롬프트 (ChatGPT)

> 자동 생성 — `node tools/gen-roster-doc.mjs`

## 공통 스타일 (모든 프롬프트 앞에 붙는다)

```
Pixel art game sprite of a chibi character with exactly 2-head-tall proportions: the head is exactly half of the total height (head top to chin = chin to feet). Every character in this series uses the SAME head size and the same frame: the head is a large rounded shape about 50% of the sprite height, eyes at the middle of the head, short stubby body and legs. Hats, crowns and hair may sit on top of the head but stay small; weapons are held at the side and do not stretch the height. Full body standing pose, 3/4 front view facing right, JRPG fantasy style, big visible square pixels scaled up with nearest neighbor, very low resolution with few details, 1-pixel dark outline, flat colors, max 10 colors, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no dithering, plain flat pure white background, single character centered, no text
```

## 사용 방법

1. 아래 프롬프트를 ChatGPT 에 한 명씩 넣는다 (한 번에 여러 명을 넣으면 크기·스타일이 섞인다)
2. 결과 PNG 를 저장소 `art-inbox/` 폴더에 `캐릭터id.png` 이름으로 올린다 (예: `eric.png`)
3. Claude 가 `tools/import-art.py` 로 16x16 격자 정리·배경 제거·색 줄이기를 하고 게임에 넣는다
